Illustrative weekly status: website migration

Overall: At risk. Cutover readiness depends on closure of a blocking redirect defect.

Completed: Core page validation and publishing-workflow review.

Next: Retest corrected redirect behavior, complete locale checks, and review the cutover runbook.

Decision needed: At the readiness review, assess remaining defects against agreed launch criteria and confirm whether to proceed.

Owners: Platform team for the fix; web QA for retest; release stakeholders for the go/no-go decision.
